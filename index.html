Get to know us

SLIT Magazine is a print-only dyke porn magazine creating and publishing analog erotica by and for lesbians. Our mission is to protect, objectify, and liberate lesbian sexuality and community through independent printmedia.
Lesbian porn is not necessarily hard to find... However, the bulk of this media is fabricated for cis straight men- often in questionable ethical conditions- and caters to their fantasies rather than the real desires of the lesbian community. SLIT prioritizes creating content in an ethical, fully consensual environment, curated by and for fellow dykes.
Especially in the digital age, even lesbian-made content found online can feel questionable to access, and even worse to publish, because you have no control over viewership. Additionally, some of this content is often exclusionary of trans and gender-nonconforming lesbians. What makes SLIT especially important in this day and age is our print-only nature. This intention mitigates the amount of non-lesbians who engage with the product through intentional modes of distribution with people and suppliers we trust, and empowers a much-needed offline community that was once thriving but has dwindled in the age of technological isolation.
